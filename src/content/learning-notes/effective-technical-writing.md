I learned more about technical writing from the [MDN article](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/blog/technical-writing/).

- Clarity, conciseness and consistency matter.
- Keep one idea per paragraph.
- Keep sentences short.
- Use the same terminology throughout a document.

Further reading: [Readability Guidelines](https://readabilityguidelines.co.uk/).
