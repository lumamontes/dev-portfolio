"Music videos on tv" is a silly thing I do with my friends since I bought a TV with an LED strip: put on a music video, a recorded concert, a movie, a match of something, and the room's light changes color along with the screen.

This collection is just that — screens photographed in the middle of the mess of cables and colored light, each one with a soundtrack or a memory of a specific night.
