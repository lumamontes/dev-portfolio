Biblioteca de Zines is a live archive and catalogue for zines and independent publications, with a focus on the Amazon region. Recognized with the Prêmio Mapinguari, it has a volunteer editorial dashboard for reviewing, editing, publishing, and rejecting submissions — with 337 published zines, 219 artist/author profiles, and 379 submissions received so far.
