# Remove standalone About and Contact pages

The site had separate `/about` and `/contact` pages that duplicated identity and reach-out content the redesign already commits to putting on a single unified homepage (see `.scratch/personal-archive-redesign/spec.md`). We removed both as standalone destinations — bio/identity content and contact links now live on the homepage, and the old URLs redirect there — because keeping them separate re-creates exactly the recruiter-vs-artist fragmentation this redesign exists to remove.
